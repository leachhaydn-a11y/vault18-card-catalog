# Bulk Storage

This section documents your bulk card storage and organization system.

## Overview

Bulk storage is used for:
- Common and uncommon cards
- Bulk lots
- Cards awaiting organization
- Overflow from main collections

## Storage Locations

Document where your bulk cards are stored:
- Container type (box, bin, etc.)
- Location
- Contents summary
- Last updated date

## Organization Tips

- Group by card game
- Organize by color/type when applicable
- Keep inventory notes
- Regular maintenance and rotation

## Inventory

Track your bulk inventory including:
- Container ID/name
- Game type
- Approximate card count
- Notable cards (if any)
- Storage location
